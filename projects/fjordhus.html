A gimbal expansion joint built for heavy-duty piping, absorbing angular movement in any plane under full internal pressure.

Gimbal Expansion Joint allows simultaneous angular movement in multiple planes while fully containing internal pressure.

The gimbal rings are precision-forged and the hinge pins high-tensile, giving positive mechanical restraint under high-pressure and high-temperature operating conditions.

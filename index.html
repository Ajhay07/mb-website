Process

Every joint is engineered to a specification. We prove it in the shop: through correlation tests, destructive tests and type tests on every design.

Every design starts from your parameters: nominal bore, design pressure, design temperature, flow media, flow velocity, and axial, lateral and angular deflection. Each unit is then formed, welded, tested and inspected to EJMA, ASME and PED requirements before it ships.

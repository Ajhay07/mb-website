Who We Are

A specialist manufacturer, not a general fabricator.

A complete range of bellows, expansion joints, compensators, metal hoses and fabric bellows — built at a dedicated facility in the SIPCOT corridor at Oragadam, Chennai.

  • 20 mm to 9000 mm

    Stainless steel, high-nickel alloy and carbon steel bellows across the full diameter range, including instrument bellows and assemblies.

  • Designed to your parameters

    Every unit is engineered to nominal bore, design pressure and temperature, flow media and axial, lateral and angular deflection.

  • Proven under inspection

    ASME U-Stamp and PED certified, approved by boiler boards in India and overseas, and witnessed by the world's leading agencies.

Product Range

Expansion joints for every movement and medium.

Circular and rectangular, restrained and unrestrained — each type is matched to the movements, thrust and service conditions of your line.

Lateral / Axial

Universal Expansion Joint

Two bellows connected by a centre spool to absorb large lateral movement alongside axial and angular deflection — the workhorse for long pipe runs between anchors.

Enquire
Angular · Multi-plane

Gimbal Expansion Joint

A gimbal ring assembly permits angular rotation in any plane while fully restraining pressure thrust, used in sets to route piping around equipment.

Enquire
Thrust Balanced

Pressure Balanced Joint

Absorbs axial and lateral movement while balancing pressure thrust across the bellows — protecting pumps, turbines and nozzles at pipe elbows and tees.

Enquire
Tie-Rod Restrained

Tied Lateral Expansion Joint

Tie rods contain the full pressure thrust so the joint handles pure lateral deflection — proven between turbine and condenser on large power trains.

Enquire
High Pressure

Reinforced Bellow

Equalising and reinforcing rings raise the pressure rating of the convolutions, engineered for demanding high-pressure service conditions.

Enquire
Ducting · Low Pressure

Rectangular Expansion Joint

Metallic and fabric rectangular joints for flue-gas ducting, fans and low-pressure gas systems where large thermal growth must be accommodated.

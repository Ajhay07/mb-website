Who We Are

We only make bellows and expansion joints.

Bellows, expansion joints, compensators, metal hoses and fabric bellows, all made at our own plant in the SIPCOT industrial area at Oragadam, Chennai.

  • 20 mm to 9000 mm

    Stainless steel, high-nickel alloy and carbon steel bellows in every diameter from 20 mm up, plus instrument bellows and assemblies.

  • Made to your duty

    We size each unit from your nominal bore, design pressure and temperature, the medium, and the axial, lateral and angular movement it has to take.

  • Inspected by third parties

    We hold ASME U-Stamp and PED certification and approvals from boiler boards in India and abroad. Independent agencies witness our tests.

Product Range

Expansion joints for your line.

Round or rectangular, restrained or free. We match the joint to the movement, thrust and operating conditions of your pipeline.

View the full range
Lateral and axial

Universal Expansion Joint

Two bellows joined by a centre spool. It takes large lateral movement along with axial and angular movement, which makes it the usual choice for long pipe runs between anchors.

Enquire
Angular, multi-plane

Gimbal Expansion Joint

A gimbal ring lets the pipe rotate in any plane while holding the full pressure thrust. Fitted in sets, it routes piping around equipment.

Enquire
Thrust balanced

Pressure Balanced Joint

Takes axial and lateral movement and balances the pressure thrust across the bellows, so pumps, turbines and nozzles at elbows and tees are not loaded.

Enquire
Tie-rod restrained

Tied Lateral Expansion Joint

Tie rods carry the full pressure thrust, so the joint only has to take lateral movement. We have supplied these between turbines and condensers on large power trains.

Enquire
High pressure

Reinforced Bellow

Equalising and reinforcing rings raise the pressure rating of the convolutions for high-pressure service.

Enquire
Ducting, low pressure

Rectangular Expansion Joint

Metallic and fabric rectangular joints for flue gas ducts, fans and low-pressure gas systems where the duct grows a lot with heat.

Where Our Joints Run

Where our joints are installed.

From blast furnace gas mains to turbine exhausts, our bellows take up the heat growth, vibration and pressure cycling that rigid pipe cannot.

  • Turbines & compressors
  • Heat exchangers
  • Boilers & steam-extraction lines
  • Exhaust & flue-gas pipe lines
  • DG sets
  • Heat-recovery systems
  • Pre-heater cyclones

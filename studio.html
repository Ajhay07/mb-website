About

Formerly the Industrial Division of Metallic Bellows (I) Pvt Ltd, MB Metallic Bellows has grown into a team of skilled engineers and fabricators manufacturing expansion joints and bellows from our Chennai facility.

We supply bellows, expansion joints and metal hoses to power, steel, refinery, fertilizer, cement and process plants across India.

Every order is followed by our engineers from first drawing to dispatch. Special applications are designed and manufactured to your specification.

How we work

  1. Stage 01

    Engineering Design

    Every design starts with operating duty: working pressure, design temperature, media, and axial/lateral movements.

  2. Stage 02

    Forming & Welding

    The bellows element is formed on precision tooling and welded by certified welders, following procedures qualified to ASME and EJMA standards.

  3. Stage 03

    Testing & Inspection

    Every unit is pressure tested, dimensionally checked and inspected before dispatch, with documentation to ASME, ASTM, DIN or IS as required.

  4. Stage 04

    Field Third-Party Inspection

    Independent third-party inspection is available on request, and our team supports commissioning and follow-up checks in the field.

From the model workshop

Every joint starts as a drawing and a design check. Our designs are supported by correlation, destructive and type tests, so each unit performs to the parameters you specify.

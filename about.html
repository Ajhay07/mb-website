Overview

Built on engineering depth, proven in the field.

MB Metallic Bellows Private Limited (MBMBPL) is a leading manufacturer of bellows, expansion joints, compensators, metal hoses, rubber bellows, non-metallic fabric bellows, dampers, strainers, couplings and sleeve couplings — in a strategically located industrial zone at Chennai, India, with close proximity to air, sea, rail and road services.

Our bellows expansion joints absorb the movements that arise in multiple planes in pipelines due to variation in temperature and pressure. We follow the standards laid down by the Expansion Joint Manufacturers' Association, USA (EJMA), and for special applications our units are designed and manufactured to the customer's specification — supported by appropriate correlation, destructive and type tests.

How We Engineer

Designed to your parameters.

For special applications, units are designed and manufactured to the customer's specification on the basis of parameters such as:

  • Nominal bore & design pressure
  • Design temperature & flow media
  • Flow velocity
  • Axial, lateral and angular deflection

Engineers analyse each requirement using conventional calculation and computer-based simulation to arrive at the optimal flexible-element solution.

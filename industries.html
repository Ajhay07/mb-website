Industries & Clients

Used by the companies that run India's plants.

Our expansion joints are installed at national power utilities, integrated steel plants, refineries, fertilizer producers and naval dockyards.

Sectors We Serve

Flexible elements for heavy industry.

Power

Thermal and nuclear generation: turbine exhaust, boiler, extraction and heat-recovery joints.

Steel & Metals

Blast-furnace hot-blast, BF-gas mains and ducting for integrated steel plants.

Refineries & Hydrocarbons

Process piping, crude and carbon-black units in corrosion-resistant alloys.

Fertilizers & Chemicals

Aggressive-media expansion joints in high-nickel and special stainless grades.

Cement

Sugar & Process

Evaporator, vapour-line and process-fluid compensators.

Shipping & Defence

Dockyard and naval exhaust, scupper and discharge systems.

Boilers & HRSG

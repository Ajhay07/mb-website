Product Range

Bellows and expansion joints to EJMA standards.

Circular and rectangular expansion joints, metal hoses and related flexible products. Sizes run from 20 mm instrument bellows to 9 metre large-bore joints, in stainless steel, high-nickel alloy and carbon steel.

Expansion Joints

Joints matched to your pipeline.

Each type deals with a different mix of axial, lateral and angular movement. Our team will pick the right one for your line.

Lateral and axial

Universal Expansion Joint

Two bellows joined by a centre spool. It takes large lateral movement along with axial and angular movement, which makes it the usual choice for long pipe runs between anchors.

Enquire
Angular, multi-plane

Gimbal Expansion Joint

A gimbal ring lets the pipe rotate in any plane while holding the full pressure thrust. Fitted in sets, it routes piping around equipment.

Enquire
Thrust balanced

Pressure Balanced Joint

Takes axial and lateral movement and balances the pressure thrust across the bellows, so pumps, turbines and nozzles at elbows and tees are not loaded.

Enquire
Tie-rod restrained

Tied Lateral Expansion Joint

Tie rods carry the full pressure thrust, so the joint only has to take lateral movement. We have supplied these between turbines and condensers on large power trains.

Enquire
High pressure

Reinforced Bellow

Equalising and reinforcing rings raise the pressure rating of the convolutions for high-pressure service.

Enquire
Ducting, low pressure

Rectangular Expansion Joint

Metallic and fabric rectangular joints for flue gas ducts, fans and low-pressure gas systems where the duct grows a lot with heat.

Enquire
Up to 9000 mm

Large-Bore Bellow

Single-piece and segmented bellows up to 9 metres across for blast furnace gas mains, ducts and heavy process lines. We fabricate and test them in our own shop.

Blast furnace

Tuyere Stock Bellow

Expansion bellows made for the tuyere stock on a blast furnace hot-blast system, where they see extreme temperature swings and abrasion.

Complete Range

Beyond expansion joints.

MB Metallic Bellows also manufactures a broad range of related flexible and pipeline products.

Metal Hoses

Corrugated and strip-wound flexible hoses for high-pressure service, vibration isolation and pipe misalignment.

Compensators

Fabric and metallic compensators for ducting, fans and flue-gas systems handling large thermal growth.

Rubber Bellows

Elastomer expansion joints for water, effluent and low-temperature media where flexibility and damping matter.

Non-Metallic Fabric Bellows

Multi-layer fabric joints for large rectangular ducts, hot gas and low-pressure air systems.

Dampers

Flow-control and isolation dampers integrated with duct expansion elements.

Strainers

Pipeline strainers to protect pumps, valves and downstream equipment.

Couplings & Sleeve Couplings

Pipe couplings and sleeve couplings for jointing, repair and axial take-up.

Instrument Bellows & Assemblies

Precision small-bore bellows, assemblies, bellows-related ducts and gaskets.

Materials & Range

From 20 mm to 9 metres.

Bellows are formed in stainless steel, high-nickel alloys and carbon steel to suit temperature, corrosion and pressure. Both circular and rectangular geometries are fabricated and tested in-house.

  • Stainless steel, Inconel / Incoloy and carbon-steel convolutions
  • Single-ply and multi-ply constructions
  • Reinforced and equalising-ring designs for high pressure
  • Large-bore and segmented fabrication up to 9000 mm

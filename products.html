Product Range

Expansion Joints

Matched to your movements, thrust and medium.

Each type restrains or accommodates a different combination of axial, lateral and angular movement — our team specifies the right configuration for your line.

Lateral / Axial

Universal Expansion Joint

Two bellows connected by a centre spool to absorb large lateral movement alongside axial and angular deflection — the workhorse for long pipe runs between anchors.

Angular · Multi-plane

Gimbal Expansion Joint

A gimbal ring assembly permits angular rotation in any plane while fully restraining pressure thrust, used in sets to route piping around equipment.

Thrust Balanced

Pressure Balanced Joint

Absorbs axial and lateral movement while balancing pressure thrust across the bellows — protecting pumps, turbines and nozzles at pipe elbows and tees.

Enquire
Tie-Rod Restrained

Tied Lateral Expansion Joint

Tie rods contain the full pressure thrust so the joint handles pure lateral deflection — proven between turbine and condenser on large power trains.

Reinforced Bellow

Materials & Range

From 20 mm to 9 metres.

Bellows are formed in stainless steel, high-nickel alloys and carbon steel to suit temperature, corrosion and pressure. Both circular and rectangular geometries are fabricated and tested in-house.

  • Stainless steel, Inconel / Incoloy and carbon-steel convolutions
  • Single-ply and multi-ply constructions
  • Reinforced and equalising-ring designs for high pressure
  • Large-bore and segmented fabrication up to 9000 mm
